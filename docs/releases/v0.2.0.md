# Homecraft v0.2.0

This release adds the one-way door rings and the world map to the shared engine, plus an optional `doors` field in the site metadata.

- One-way doors: `DOORS` in `world/layout.js` places small gates with a glowing ground ring; stepping on one leaves for its single destination through the normal travel flow. The gate's sign and the emblem at the vortex centre load the destination's name and logo from its `homecraft.json`.
- World map: `M` opens a fullscreen relationship graph of the portal network on an old-chart parchment background. Door hops become edges, discovered recursively through each site's `doors` field; directory worlds without door links ring the outer bands. Painting is paged — dragging loads more worlds and their metadata, and the listing is re-checked on demand. Hovering a node shows its details; clicking one travels right away.
- Protocol: `homecraft.json` gains the optional `doors` array (up to 16 URLs). Older readers ignore the field, and worlds that do not publish it stay unlinked on the map — no migration needed.
- Keys and UI: `M` opens the world map, `Tab` keeps the island's own big map, and a corner button sits beside the web button.
- Checks: door-ring integration coverage in `tools/check-portal.mjs`, `doors` validation in `tools/check-metadata.mjs`, and a cross-origin image stand-in in `tools/check.mjs`.

Sites take the update into their local copy the usual way and redeploy afterwards. The manifest now covers the new `engine/ui/worldmap.js` and `engine/world/doorring.js`, so local edits to them are tracked like any other engine file.
